About the school

A driving school that takes its time with you

Most people do not struggle because driving is difficult. They struggle because somebody rushed them. At Back Gear we teach the way we would want our own family taught — calmly, in your own language, with a second set of controls on the instructor's side so nothing can go wrong.

You start on quiet roads, move into real Bathinda traffic when you are ready, and practise the actual test route before your RTO appointment.

  • Dual-control training vehicles
  • Learner licence and driving licence paperwork handled for you
  • Choose your own days and time slot
  • Ladies' batch with a lady instructor

Licence help

We handle the RTO paperwork

Applying for a licence means forms, documents and appointments. We do that part with you, so you only have to concentrate on driving.

  1. 1
    Learner licence

    Documents checked, Form 1 filled, slot booked and practice for the online test.

  2. 2
    Your classes

    Practical lessons on a schedule that suits you, with your attendance tracked from day one.

  3. 3
    Driving test

    Practice on the real test track, then we come with you on the day of the test.

  4. 4
    Driving licence

    We follow the application through until the licence is in your hand.
