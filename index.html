        06 / BEFORE YOU GET GOING

        A few things
        you might
        be wondering.

        New to all of this? That’s completely okay.

        Let’s talk it through ↗
        Can I join if I’ve never driven before?

        Absolutely. Our beginner course starts with the controls, moving off and stopping. You’ll build up to road practice at a pace that feels comfortable.

        Can I fit classes around work or college?

        We offer morning and evening slots, Monday to Saturday. Choose a preferred time in the booking form and the school will confirm availability.

        Do I need my own car?

        No. Training vehicles are provided, with dual controls for car lessons. Speak to the school about manual or automatic availability.

        Will you help me get my driving licence?

        We offer guidance on documents, learner licence applications and test preparation. The RTO makes the final decision on granting a licence.

        How much does a course cost?

        Fees depend on your course and the number of lessons. Request a callback for the full price before you decide. There is no payment to enquire.

        Is pickup available in my area?

        Pickup assistance is available across most of Bathinda. Add your area to the booking form so the school can check the route with you.
